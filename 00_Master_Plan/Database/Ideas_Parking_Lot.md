# FootballPlatform Ideas Parking Lot

## Scouting & Player Analysis

### 1. Competition Strength Index

A system for comparing the strength of different football competitions.

Purpose:
- Put player statistics into competition context.
- Help compare performances between different leagues.
- Support future scouting recommendations.

---

### 2. Playing Time / Involvement Indicator

Calculate player involvement based on minutes played compared with available team minutes.

Possible display:

- 🟢 High involvement
- 🟡 Medium involvement
- 🔴 Low involvement

Purpose:
Make playing time immediately understandable without requiring the scout to calculate percentages manually.

---

### 3. Competition DNA

Create a profile for each competition using characteristics such as:

- Tempo
- Physicality
- Goals per game
- Average player age
- Youth opportunity
- Other statistical characteristics

---

### 4. Hidden Gems

Identify players who perform strongly relative to:

- Competition strength
- Age
- Playing time
- Position
- Market value
- Other contextual factors

---

### 5. League-to-League Translation

Estimate how a player's performance in one competition might translate to another competition with a different level of difficulty.
